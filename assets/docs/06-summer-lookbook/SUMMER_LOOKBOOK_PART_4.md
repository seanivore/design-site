# Summer Lookbook Flow

## Section 10: Timeless Custodians

**TIMELESS CONFIDENCE**

- Designing for lovers of tradition who need a dash of modern.
- Guardians of our sacred, time-honored rituals.
- They command respect through their adherence to principles and discipline, helping them maintain a steady course amidst turbulent times.
- Preserving values that have withstood the test of time is a tall task, even as it provides a sense of purpose and grounding.
- However, their rigid adherence to tradition can lead to conflict internally or with others during periods that require progress. The goal here is to find balance between maintaining the status quo and adapting a changing world.

**Neoclassicism x Art Deco**

- Embody values of style, grace, and poise and integrating current style trends.
- Ensembles convey refined taste and understated sophistication. ‍
- Neoclassicism inspired elegant silhouettes of balance, proportion, harmony. ‍
- Use Art Deco's clean lines and geometry to emphasize appreciation for structure, discipline, and enduring classic design.‍
- Convey quiet authority via rich, deep hues like navy, burgundy, forest green — and neutral tones like ivory, taupe, and gray. ‍
- Use quality textiles like fine wool, silk, cashmere, linen, with fitted silhouettes tailored impeccably.
- Reflect ability to balance tradition and adaptability with subtle modern updates.
- Pieces should be easily dressed up or down using layering for versatility.

## Section 11: For Dreamweavers

**STYLING IMAGINATIVE REALITY**

- Attire for personality living between worlds of imagination and reality.
- They challenge conventions, creating progress and change.
- Enchanting and whimsical energy inspires others to embrace their inner child and view the world through a lens of wonder and possibility.
- Vivid, boundless imagination allows them to conjure visions of worlds unseen; their mind dancing among the clouds and infusing the ordinary with a touch of magic.  
- Constant oscillation between fantasy and practicality is exhausting — strategically produced wardrobe can encourage imagination while helping encourage balance between the ethereal and the tangible.

**Surrealism x Pre-Raphaelite**

- Encourage creativity from a blurred fantasy and reality — while grounding.
- Ensembles evoke enchanting intricate tapestries of meaning. ‍
- Surrealism in ethereal, dreamlike silhouettes that encourage transcending boundaries.
- Romantic, flowing Lines inspired by Pre-Raphaelite nature-inspired motifs, mythical beauty.‍
- Evoke movement in ethereal textiles like diaphanous fabrics chiffon, organza, and tulle.‍
- Break mundane constraints using unstructured silhouettes that encourage movement and freedom with billowing sleeves, cascading ruffles, asymmetrical hemlines.
- Trompe l'oeil prints, embroidery, and transformative garments play into blurring reality and fantasy.
- Use transparency and texture, pieces that reveal and conceal, layer in depth.

## Section 12: For the Empath

**SOFT, GROUNDED FLOW**

- Curating wardrobe for personality types in need of grounding, comfort, and balance.
- Profoundly sensitive and compassionate, Empaths offer deep understanding and comfort.
- They possess an innate understanding of the depths of the human experience making them able to sense the unspoken currents that flow beneath the surface.
- Through this gentle and nurturing nature they radiate a sense of safety and sanctuary for those who seek their counsel.
- Constant absorption of emotional energies can create overwhelm and exhaustion, as they navigate the delicate balance between their own well-being and their desire to be a source of support for others. 

**Romanticism x Impressionism**

- Attire brings this personality type much needed grounding.
- Ensembles cultivate inner calm and create graceful movement.
- Soft, flowing silhouettes provide comfort — inspired by introspection of Romanticism.
- Delicate, painterly prints emphasize shifting energies and ephemeral beauty of Impressionism.
- Combine warm neutrals and pops of subdued emotive hues like dusty rose and deep plum.
- Prioritize comfort textiles, e.g. cotton, linen, knits to provide grounding.
- Add meaning with symbolic jewelry, handcrafted pieces with a story.
- Choose shawls for comfort and protection.
- Unstructured silhouettes and ease of movement; dresses, oversized sweaters, loose-fitting trousers.
