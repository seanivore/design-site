# Summer Lookbook Flow

## Section 7: For the Architect

**DRESSING MULTIFACETED MAGIC**

- Curating attire to help personalities with hyper-focus feel less detached from what's around them.
- Admired for their deep sense of purpose, drive for perfection, and unwavering focus.
- They achieve meticulously crafted goals and find satisfaction in the pursuit of intellectual challenges.
- An analytical mind that is constantly seeking out patterns, connections, and innovative solutions — an energy of quiet intensity.
- Commanding respect through their strategic acumen and ability to navigate complex systems, however, single-mindedness for their objectives can lead to feelings of isolation from the emotional undercurrents that surround them.

**Bauhaus x Constructivism**

- Get loud without looking loud.
- Ensembles are streamlined, minimalist, bold statements.
- Reflect love of structure, functionality with clean, geometric Bauhaus inspired silhouettes.
- Appreciation for innovation and tech by incorporating industrial materials, bold angular Constructivism inspired lines.
- Balance analytical nature with adaptability via contrasting color palettes of strong hues (black, white, primary colors) beside muted tones (gray, navy, olive).
- Durable, refined textiles combined with luxurious merino wool, silk, or leather.
- Tailored, precise, silhouettes showcase attention to detail.
- Layering and adaptability allow Architect to adjust their look to suit changing environments.

## Section 8: Metamorphs

**EVER EVOLVING STYLE**

- Clothing a personality that is in a constant state of flux feels, kind of, exhilarating.
- Electric and ever-changing energy inspires others to challenge their own preconceptions.
- The Metamorph’s experiences a kaleidoscope of feelings. They shift and evolve as they navigate this process of their transformation.
- Embracing the unconventional and finding exhilaration in pushing boundaries, they're always hungry for new experiences to fuel a profound sense of growth and self-discovery.
- However, their constant state of reinvention can sometimes lead to feelings of instability, a longing for moments of grounding amidst the chaos of change.

**Neo-Expressionism x Surrealism**

- Wardrobe that illustrates (or just accompanies) the journey of self discovery.
- Ensembles are eclectic, individualistic, vintage.
- Willingness to embrace unconventional, expressive, dynamic, Neo-Expressionism silhouettes.
- Metamorph’s ability to challenge perceptions and blur the lines using dreamlike Surrealist imagery.
- Illustrate their desire to stand out with bold, vibrant colors, clashing hues, neon accents.
- Hunger for new sensations via textiles, combining iridescent with organics. ‍
- Conversational starter accessories. Gender-fluid silhouettes challenge expectations. ‍
- Layering and deconstruction with garments that can be worn in multiple ways or reveal hidden details — a complex, reflection of a multifaceted nature.

## Section 9: For Voyagers

**BLISS-FILLED WANDERLUST**

- Dressing to empower navigating balance between exploration and stability.
- Unwavering optimism and insatiable wanderlust inspires those around them.
- With a zest for life that propels them towards new horizons, they approach each day with a sense of excitement and anticipation.
- With a vibrant and free-spirited nature, their energy and love of adventure comes off as infectious enthusiasm that draws others into their orbit.
- Their willingness to immerse themselves in new experiences, however can sometimes lead to moments of overindulgence.

**Psychedelic Art x Art Nouveau**

- Design for a forever young self-expression, while still grounding strong personality.
- Ensembles are layered, eclectic, and pull off combining disparate elements. ‍
- Psychedelic Art inspired vibrant, kaleidoscopic color palettes.
- Show beauty of freedom with organic, Art Nouveau nature-inspired motifs. ‍
- Embrace eclectic bohemian silhouettes with multicultural elements.
- Intricate embroidery, original artisanal pieces, hand dyed fabrics speak to this personality.
- Every piece should carry a story.
- Comfort and ease of movement — with breathable, natural fabrics and relaxed.
- Bold, statement-making prints and patterns — reflecting willingness to stand out and make a unique impression wherever their journey takes them.
